Available images for VtLinux layers are:

  - Official VtLinux images:
      vtlinux-image-core       -   VtLinux core image
      vtlinux-image-qt         -   VtLinux qt image

  - Other VtLinux images:
      - Supported images:
          vtlinux-image-weston    -   VtLinux weston image

  - Maybe factory will use image:
          vtlinux-image-factory   -   Factory image for burning

  - ThermaSol product images (single-app Qt5 EGLFS kiosk):
          vtlinux-image-signatouch     -   SignaTouch (steam/shower/light/music,
                                           rotatable display, room temp sensor)
          vtlinux-image-signatouch-ir  -   SignaTouch IR (infrared sauna)
